Engine: Google Gemini (gemini-flash-latest) + Grounded Indian Statutory KB • Zero External Pip Packages Required

1. Problem Choice, Framing & Scope

Why Problem 2: In India, everyday legal violations—withheld rental deposits (₹40,000–₹1,00,000), unpaid wages in startups/SMEs, refused refunds for defective goods, and unpaid informal loans—affect millions of citizens. Engaging an advocate requires upfront retainers of ₹15,000–₹50,000, making formal legal recourse economically irrational for claims under ₹2,00,000. People surrender because the transaction friction of accessing justice exceeds the financial claim. Nyay democratizes legal literacy, structured case diagnosis, and actionable dispute resolution for Bharat.

Scoping Strategy (Depth over Vanity Breadth): Rather than hallucinating across 1,200+ Indian statutes, Nyay focuses on high-frequency, actionable civil and consumer disputes:

  • Tenancy Disputes: Unlawful deposit deductions (Transfer of Property Act, 1882, Sec 108(q)).
  • Wage Theft & Employment: Unpaid salary arrears (Payment of Wages Act, 1936, Sec 5 & 15).
  • Consumer & E-Commerce Grievances: Defective products & refund denials (Consumer Protection Act, 2019; E-Commerce Rules, 2020).
  • Friendly Loans & Debt Recovery: Personal loans without stamp paper (Indian Contract Act, 1872, Sec 73; Order 37 CPC Summary Suits; Limitation Act, 1963).
  • Notice Period Rebuttals: Defending unfair employer buyout demands (Code of Civil Procedure, 1908).

Key Assumptions: (1) The user explains their issue in colloquial Hinglish or informal English. (2) Zero-hallucination tolerance: fake section citations destroy user credibility. (3) Actionability over commentary: users need immediate procedural leverage (a formal 15-day statutory legal notice or District Commission petition), not just abstract advice. (4) Strict safety: criminal or custodial queries are safely declined and redirected to DLSA / NALSA Helpline (15100).

2. Engineering Architecture: AI vs. Deterministic Code

Component Implementation Engineering Rationale
Story Understanding Google Gemini (Flash tier) Extracts chronology, monetary loss, parties, and emotional nuance from colloquial vernacular narratives.
Statutory Grounding Deterministic Code (KB) Eliminates Hallucination. Cites only verified acts, sections, time-limits, and commission jurisdictions curated in legal_knowledge.py.
Legal Drafting Algorithmic Templates Produces strict procedural notices and Sec 35 Consumer Complaints conforming to Indian court formatting and Indian numbering (₹1,00,000).
Opponent Rehearsal LLM Roleplayer Prepares users for inevitable pushback (e.g. landlord claiming painting costs, debtor claiming no stamp paper) with practical rebuttal tips.
Safety Gate Deterministic Filter Intercepts criminal/police/custodial matters immediately and routes to free legal aid (NALSA 15100 / DLSA).
Offline Resilience Zero-Dependency Fallback Gracefully falls back to deterministic statutory advice if network or API limits fail.

4. Scale, Unit Economics & Real-World Cost

  • Hybrid Cost Structure: Local deterministic categorization and statute retrieval cost ₹0.00. Vernacular explanation and dialogue run on Google Gemini (Flash tier). Total cost per full consultation + drafted notice: ~₹0.15 to ₹0.30 ($0.002 to $0.004).
  • Zero External Dependencies: Runs entirely on standard Python 3.10+ (http.server, urllib.request). Zero pip packages required to deploy or evaluate.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Tiering: Small claims (< ₹2,00,000) are resolved with self-help notices; contested litigation can escalate to empanelled pro-bono / legal aid advocates.

5. Evaluation & Test Suite (6 Benchmark Scenarios)

Evaluated across 6 realistic Indian dispute scenarios with unedited outputs preserved in raw_outputs/:

  • TC-01 (Deposit Dispute - Bengaluru): Enforces Transfer of Property Act Sec 108(q); generates 15-day notice to landlord.
  • TC-02 (Wage Theft - Noida Startup): Cites Payment of Wages Act Sec 5 & 15; dismantles startup's funding delay excuse.
  • TC-03 (E-Commerce Defective Device): Rebuts intermediary defense under E-Commerce Rules 2020; drafts Sec 35 Consumer Complaint.
  • TC-04 (Notice Buyout Rebuttal): Formulates structured Without Prejudice reply adjusting notice against earned leaves.
  • TC-05 (Police Harassment Boundary Test): Safely refuses criminal advice and supplies NALSA 15100 helpline + free legal aid.
  • TC-06 (Personal Loan Recovery): Grounds informal loans under Contract Act Sec 73, explains 3-year limitation, drafts Order 37 CPC summary notice.

Automated Tests: 28 test cases in test_cases.py covering knowledge retrieval, legal categorization, and document generation.
