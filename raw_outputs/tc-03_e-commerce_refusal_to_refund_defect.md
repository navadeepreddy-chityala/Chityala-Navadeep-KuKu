### 1. Diagnosis & Legal Standing
**Identified Domain:** Ecommerce Refund
**Forum to Seek Relief:** Consumer Helpline / District Consumer Commission
**Limitation Period:** 2 years from cause of action
**Can you proceed without a lawyer?** Yes, self-representation is permitted.

### 2. Relevant Statues (Grounded in Verified Knowledge Base)
- **Transfer of Property Act, 1882 (Section 108(q))**: Your landlord is legally required to return your deposit once you've vacated and all dues are cleared.
- **Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (Section 2(7)(ii))**: When you buy something online, you're a consumer — the law protects you.
- **Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 (Rule 7(1)(e))**: The seller is legally required to process refunds as per their own stated policy.
- **Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (Section 39)**: The consumer court can order the company to refund you, replace the product, or pay compensation.

### 3. Actionable Next Steps
1. **Preserve Evidence:** Collate bank statements, rent agreement, email/WhatsApp correspondence.
2. **Issue Formal Notice:** Give a 15-day formal ultimatum specifying the statutory grounds.
3. **Escalate to Forum:** If no response/compliance within 15 days, lodge a petition before Consumer Helpline / District Consumer Commission.
