### 2. Relevant Statues (Grounded in Verified Knowledge Base)
- **Code of Civil Procedure, 1908 (Section 80)**: A legal notice is NOT a court order — it's a formal letter. You have time to respond.
- **Limitation Act, 1963 (Section 2(j))**: Just because you received a notice doesn't mean you've lost. The other party still has to prove their claim in court.
